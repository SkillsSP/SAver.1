About us

A center, not a school. Skills, not just grades.

Methodology

A program built on research, not intuition

The core premise: a child is not a passive recipient of knowledge, but an active participant in the learning process. Hence the six methods behind every class.

Total Physical Response (TPR)

James Asher's method — a tight link between language, movement and action. Children respond to language by performing actions, mirroring how a first language is naturally acquired. The result: less stress, more engagement, faster retention.

Project Based Learning (PBL)

Learning through real projects — children solve problems, collaborate, plan and present their own work. Builds creativity, independence and social skills.

Storytelling

Story as a teaching tool — it builds language context, grows imagination, and strengthens memory.

Music-Based Learning

Rhythm, singing and rhymes support language memory, pronunciation, phonological awareness and motivation.

Experiential Learning

Learning by doing — children gain knowledge through experimentation, observation and hands-on action.

Media Literacy Education

Media literacy prepares children to navigate the internet, social media and artificial intelligence with awareness.

Program direction aligned with recommendations from the OECD, UNESCO, UNICEF, WHO and the Education Endowment Foundation (EEF) on future skills and media literacy.
