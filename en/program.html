A Core for everyone. Electives by interest.

Every membership starts with the Core — mandatory life skills and safety, delivered in English. Electives are added on top, chosen for the child, not the other way around.

Program architecture

Two Core pillars + optional electives

Every class — in the Core and in the electives — is taught entirely in English.

Pillar I · Core

Useful Skills

Practical, everyday life skills matched to age — from folding clothes to meal planning and budgeting pocket money.

Pillar II · Core

Life Skills

Response patterns for real situations — physical and digital safety, first aid, spotting manipulation and fake news.

Pillar III · Core (Teens Junior)

Exams

Preparation for Poland's 8th-grade English exam (level A2) — official past papers, task strategies, easing exam stress.

Age groups

What your child actually does in the Core

5–7

Group 1 — the transitional phase

Useful Skills: tidying up, folding clothes, sewing on a big button under supervision, watering plants.
Life Skills: "what do I do if I get lost" role-play, memorizing personal contact details, recognizing emergency services, first online safety rules.

8–10

Group 2 — missions and challenges

Useful Skills: making a simple snack, first independent sewing, organizing a backpack and workspace.
Life Skills: calling for help and the emergency number, safe device and internet use, recognizing advertising as manipulation, basic first aid.

11–12

Group 3 — a partner tone

Useful Skills: cooking simple recipes, minor repairs (hammer, screwdriver, glue), planning household tasks independently.
Life Skills: fake news and information manipulation, social media privacy, basic pre-medical first aid, peer pressure and assertiveness.

13–15

Teens Junior — goal: the 8th-grade exam

Useful Skills: more complex meals and menu planning, first budgeting, independent minor repairs.
Life Skills: digital footprint and data privacy, critical evaluation of AI-generated content, simulations of difficult situations.
Exams: grammar and vocabulary at the official exam level, timed past-paper simulations, exam stress reduction.

Electives

Chosen by interest, never assigned top-down

Open to Kids and Teens Junior as an add-on to the membership — the more electives, the lower the per-class rate.

Music Skills

Singing and translating songs, first steps on a simple instrument, rhythm as support for language memory.

Art Skills

Drawing and painting described in English, sculpture, projects tied to Core themes.

Acting Skills

Improvisation and role-play, diction exercises, expressing meaning through body language and expression.

Motion Skills

Gymnastics, acrobatics, dance, team games with instructions and rules in English.

Add-on service · fully online

American online school

Regular conversations with native-speaker instructors from the USA. This isn't a program leading to an American diploma — it's about contact with living language, accent and US cultural context, on top of in-person classes.

Who it's for

  • Students on every track: Kids, Teens Junior and Teens Senior
  • As a paid add-on to an in-person membership
  • As a standalone offer for students outside Szczecin — the online format removes the geographic barrier
  • Small groups or one-on-one, in afternoon and evening hours

Membership model

One membership, flexible content

The Core is the mandatory base of every membership. Each added elective lowers the per-class rate — a model already proven in Poland's after-school market (dance schools, sports academies).
