Not another language school

A Future Skills Competency Center for Children

We don't drill vocabulary lists. We combine English with life skills, media literacy and experiential learning — because together they prepare children for a world where they'll need to act, not just pass a test.

Acting, not memorizing

Movement, missions and play as a natural path to real communication.

Life skills

Independence and responsibility practiced hands-on, in English.

Media awareness

Online safety, fake news and AI — addressed early, not left for later.

Research-based method

TPR, PBL and Experiential Learning — recommended by the OECD, UNESCO and EEF.

Program

A Core for everyone, Electives by interest

The mandatory Core (Useful Skills + Life Skills, plus Exams for older groups) is the base of every membership plan. Electives — Music, Art, Acting or Motion Skills — are added on top, chosen for the child, not imposed from above.

Think & Speak Missions

Original missions where children solve a concrete problem using English as a tool — not as a subject in a grade book.

Airport Mission

Check-in, questions, a lost bag — communication under time pressure.

Detective Mission

Journalist Mission

Gathering information, asking questions, writing a short piece.

Scientist Mission

Method

Six methods, one goal: natural action in English

A program built on proven educational methods, aligned with directions recommended by the OECD, UNESCO, UNICEF, WHO and EEF.

Total Physical Response

Language, movement and action combined the way native speech is naturally acquired.

Project Based Learning

Children plan, collaborate and present the results of their own work.

Storytelling

Story as language context, memory and imagination in one.

Music-Based Learning

Rhythm and songs support pronunciation and language memory.

Experiential Learning

Knowledge gained through experimenting and hands-on action.

Media Literacy Education

Fake news, advertising, AI and online safety — by design, not by accident.
