Why a separate track

English isn't an add-on to the matura — it's one of the two most common choices

English is a mandatory subject at the basic level (B1). Extended-level English (B2) is one of the most commonly chosen additional subjects on Poland's matura exam — more common than math or geography. That calls for focused, dedicated preparation, not general language classes.

Syllabus

What the preparation actually covers

Basic level

B1

  • Curriculum vocabulary required by the syllabus
  • Listening and reading comprehension — official exam format
  • Short written forms: email, message, notice
  • Systematic work on recent official past papers
Extended level

B2

  • Essays and articles — structure, argumentation, evaluation
  • Listening comprehension at extended level
  • Advanced vocabulary and grammar structures
  • Time-management strategies for exam day
